
# Task 8: Simple Sales Dashboard Design

Data Analyst Internship — DataX Labs

## Objective

Build a basic interactive dashboard showing sales performance by product, region, and month.

## Tool used

Power BI

## Dataset

`Superstore_Sales.csv` — Order Date, Region, Category, Sub-Category, Sales, Profit (Jan 2023 – Dec 2024, 1,450 orders)

## What's in the dashboard

- **Line chart** — Sales by month
- **Bar chart** — Sales by region
- **Donut chart** — Sales by category
- **Slicer** — Filter by region
- **Stat cards** — Total sales, total profit, total orders, top region

## Key insights

1. West region leads with $168.6K in sales — about 62% more than East and over 3x South — contributing roughly 34% of total company sales.
2. West region sales rose 73% from Q2 2024 ($19.5K) to Q3 2024 ($33.8K), a strong seasonal spike worth targeting.
3. Technology holds the highest profit margin at 20.8%, compared to 12.2% for Office Supplies and just 3.9% for Furniture — 37% of Furniture orders sold at a loss.
4. November–December drove roughly 23% of Technology's full-year sales, in line with holiday-season electronics demand.
